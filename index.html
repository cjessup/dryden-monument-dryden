Welcome to Dryden Monument Company

Dryden Monument Company was established in the fall of 1982 to serve the memorial needs of families in Central New York State. With the modest beginning of operating from the grounds of Perkins Funeral Home in Dryden, NY we have now grown to be recognized as a major player in the memorial industry and operate out of our offices and warehouse located at 57 West Main Street, Dryden that were completed in 1986. Call us today at 607-844-8161 for any questions you may have. Check out this interesting article about Veterans markers made in America.
