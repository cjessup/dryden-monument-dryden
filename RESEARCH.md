# Dryden Monument Company — research notes

Live site reviewed 23 November 2026: [https://www.drydenmonument.com/](https://www.drydenmonument.com/)

## What the business is

Dryden Monument Company is a memorial monument dealer in Dryden, NY. The live site describes granite memorial monuments, cemetery headstones, mausoleums, bronze and granite memorial markers, pet memorials, engraving and headstone etching, cleaning of granite memorials and architecture features, custom sandblasting, and granite steps (The First Presbyterian Church of Dryden and the original entrance to The Southworth Library). The showroom, offices, and warehouse are at 57 West Main Street. The company was established in the fall of 1982 on the grounds of Perkins Funeral Home. The offices and warehouse were completed in 1986. Phone on every live page: 607-844-8161. No published email address appears on the live pages.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photographs, or branding were copied.

### 1. Sierra Headstone & Monument — https://www.sierraheadstone.com/

A family monument shop (Reno, established 1979) with a quiet, photographic home page.

Borrowed:

- Memorial photography carries the first screen, and the words sit with the photograph instead of inside a crowded sidebar.
- Families are offered the real product types as a short, numbered-feeling set of choices. Dryden’s own set is used: Monuments, Mausoleums, Engraving Services, Memorial Markers, Pet Memorials.
- A plain “how the work proceeds” moment, limited to Dryden’s own sentence: an initial conference with a memorial counselor, then a computer aided design. Sierra’s five-step process, published prices, and warranties are not used.
- Trust is a few facts the business already states (fall of 1982, 1986 building, showroom address, hours), not a row of invented counts.

### 2. Gaulden Monuments — https://www.gauldenmonuments.com/

A long-running Carolina monument company with reviews and a phone call near the top.

Borrowed:

- A Google reviews block high on the home page: aggregate stars, a count, short quotation cards, and a button out to Google.
- The phone number is a repeated, obvious action (header, reviews area, mobile bar), which matches a shop that already says “call us today.”
- Category entrances are a photograph plus a short label, not a long card of marketing copy.

Not borrowed: quote wizard, coupons, factory-direct prices, or any Gaulden review text.

### 3. Peterson Monuments, Inc. — https://petersonmonuments.com/

A full-service monument shop (since 1923 in their own telling) that leads with product groups, services for existing stones, and a large gallery of finished work.

Borrowed:

- Services and products are compact lists (chips here) rather than tall rounded cards.
- The home page shows finished work and asks people to visit the display. Dryden’s own wording is the indoor and outdoor displays and “Visit our showroom.”
- Engraving of an existing monument is its own page, separate from ordering a new monument, which is already how Dryden’s navigation works.

Not borrowed: Peterson’s extra lines (statuary, gold leaf, vault lids), its founding year, or its photographs.
